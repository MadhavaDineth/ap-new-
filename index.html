How it works

Four steps, start to receipt

  1. 1

    Fill in the patient details

    Name, address and contact number. If the patient has been here before, the record is picked up automatically.

  2. 2

    Choose dentist, treatment and time

    Only free slots are offered, so two patients can never be booked into the same chair.

  3. 3

    Get the appointment number

    A number like APT-1042 is issued, and a confirmation e-mail goes out to the patient.

  4. 4

    Settle the bill on the way out

    Treatment cost and consultation fee are totalled, and the receipt can be printed or saved as PDF.
